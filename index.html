Interactive demo

Steer the model with your keyboard, or with the on-screen pad on a touch screen, and drag a body part to pull it off balance: the model has to catch itself. Both policies run on your device: one drives the 339 muscles of the human model, the other drives the prosthesis knee and ankle from the leg’s own sensors. MuJoCo physics runs in the page as WebAssembly.
